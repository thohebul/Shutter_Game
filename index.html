HOW TO PLAY

🚀

MOVE YOUR SHIP

Use WASD or Arrow Keys on desktop. On mobile, use the movement controls.

🎯

DESTROY ENEMIES

Shoot incoming enemy ships before they reach your position.

❤️

PROTECT YOUR HULL

Avoid enemy bullets and collisions. If hull reaches zero, the mission ends.

⚡

COLLECT POWER-UPS

Power-ups can increase fire rate, shield your ship or restore health.

👹

DEFEAT THE BOSS

Later missions introduce powerful bosses with unique attack patterns.

🏆

BUILD YOUR SCORE

Destroy enemies quickly, survive longer and beat your personal high score.
